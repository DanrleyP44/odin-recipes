Homemade Vanilla Pudding

Description

I love pudding and this homemade pudding with vanilla is a rich and delectable dessert. There is no substitute for the butter!

Ingredients

  • 1/3 cup white sugar
  • 3 tablespoons cornstarch
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 2 cups whole milk
  • 1 tablespoon butter
  • 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
